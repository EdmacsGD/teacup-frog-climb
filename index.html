How to play

You are a frog in a teacup. You cannot walk or jump. The tool is all you have.

Mouse

MoveSwing the tool. The head goes where the crosshair is. ClickLock the mouse for precise control (Esc unlocks and pauses).

Keyboard

WASD / arrowsMove the crosshair. Hold Shift to move it fast. Esc PPause RRestart from the bottom (asks first) HSwitch tool: pickaxe or hammer KReturn to your checkpoint (when checkpoints are on) VSee the whole tower (or the tower button at the top) MMute FFullscreen T / 1-6Art style CShow hitboxes: exactly what collides

Touch

DragDrag anywhere to move the tool. Lift your finger and it stays where it is. ButtonsPause, sound and fullscreen are in the top right corner.

Climbing tips

  • Hook: put the head on top of a ledge, then pull the tool toward you.
  • Push up: press the head into the floor below you to lift yourself.
  • Pogo: plant the head under you and flick it down fast to launch.
  • Row: drag the head along the floor behind you to scoot forward.
  • Hang still: stop moving and you stay put. Slow hands beat fast hands.
  • The crosshair turns into a green ring while the tool has a grip.
